About Journal Labs

Welcome to my network engineering portfolio.

This site documents my practical journey through networking, Cisco configuration, Packet Tracer labs, troubleshooting, routing, switching, VLANs, DHCP, static routing, wireless access, and the wider skills I am developing while working towards CCNA-level knowledge.

The purpose of this portfolio is to show not only completed lab work, but also the process behind it: the mistakes, corrections, verification commands, design decisions, and evidence that each network behaves as intended.
